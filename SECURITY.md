# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" (private advisory) on the affected repo,
or open a `security` issue without exploit details. Do not post secrets or customer data in issues or PRs.
Secrets never go in git. Secret scanning and push protection are on for all repos.
Response targets: S1 acknowledge in 5 minutes, S2 same day, S3 within the iteration, S4 backlog.
